What We Do

The Full Build. Done Right.

Real construction work, finished properly. Every job permitted, inspected, and carried from start to finish by Cousin Charlie's Construction.

1 Full Builds

Remodels & Additions

Kitchens, bathrooms, full home remodels, room additions, second-story builds, garage conversions, and mother-in-law suites. Cabinets, tile, countertops, fixtures, paint, electrical, plumbing. One company, every step of the work.

2 Specialty

Interior and Exterior Painting

Painting done the right way, inside and out. Proper prep, clean lines, even coats, and the right paint for the job. From a single accent wall to a full exterior repaint, we finish what we start with no half-painted handoffs.

Specialty Service
3 Craft Work

Custom Cabinetry & Finish Work

Kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, entertainment built-ins, mantles, crown molding, wainscoting, and trim work. The finish details that turn a job from done to done right, all built and installed by our team.

4 Outdoor

Fencing, Patios & Outdoor Builds

Fences, slabs, driveways, walkways, patios, decks, sheds, pergolas, and outdoor structures. Concrete poured and finished by hand with proper reinforcement. Fences built square, sealed, and set to hold up through Texas weather.

5 Ground-Up Builds

New Home Builds

Ground-up custom home builds for clients who want something other than a tract builder. We handle design coordination, permitting, framing, and the full build out from foundation to finishes. If you've got the plans and the property, we've got the build.

6 Specialty

Electrical Work

Licensed electrician on staff for kitchen and bath rewires, panel upgrades, lighting installs, outlet additions, EV charger installs, and full rewires for older homes. Wiring handled by our team, permitted and inspected every time.

Specialty Service
7 Licensed Trade

Plumbing

Licensed plumber on staff for bathroom remodels, kitchen reroutes, water line replacements, fixture installs, and repairs. Plumbing handled by our team, no waiting on a third party to get your job moving.

8 Mechanical

HVAC

AC installs, replacements, ductwork, and mechanical work. Available as part of remodels, additions, new builds, or standalone jobs. One contract through us, one team coordinating the work, one finished result.

9 Top to Bottom

Roofing

Roof installs and roof repairs. New roofs on remodels, additions, and new builds, plus repair work on existing homes. Permitted, inspected, and tied into the rest of the job so you're not chasing separate contractors.

OSHA 30 certified and equipped for commercial tenant build-outs and light commercial new construction. Same single-point-of-contact approach as our residential work, scaled for business owners.
