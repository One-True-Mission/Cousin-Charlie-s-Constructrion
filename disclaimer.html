5. Licensing in Texas

Texas does not require or issue a state-level general contractor license for residential construction. This is a fact about Texas law, not a deficiency of Cousin Charlie's Construction. Many homeowners aren't aware of this when they ask about contractor licensing.

What Cousin Charlie's Construction holds:

  • Licensed electrician on staff. Our electrical work is performed by a licensed electrician credentialed through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR).
  • Licensed plumber on staff. Our plumbing work is performed by a licensed plumber credentialed through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners.
  • OSHA 30 certification. Charlie holds the 30-hour OSHA Construction Safety certification.
  • General liability and workers' compensation insurance. Cousin Charlie's Construction maintains current insurance coverage for our work and our team.

Where local jurisdictions (city or county) require permits, registrations, or specialty licenses for specific work, we pull those permits and obtain those credentials before starting work.
