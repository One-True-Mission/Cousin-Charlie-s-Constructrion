Who We Are

Family-Run. Fully Insured. Built on Doing It Right.

Cousin Charlie's Construction is a family-run shop based in Friendswood, serving homeowners across Houston, Conroe, and Galveston. We take on everything from full kitchen and bath remodels to concrete pours, soffit and fascia work, painting, and fix-and-flip projects.

No subcontractor runaround. No surprise charges. No corner-cutting. Just clean, careful work on every job, big or small. If we said we'd be there, we're there. If we said we'd handle it, it's handled.
