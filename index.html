Who We Are

Family-Run. Fully Insured. Built on Doing It Right.

Cousin Charlie's Construction is a family-run construction company based in Friendswood, serving homeowners and businesses across Houston, Conroe, and Galveston. With 15 years of construction experience and a full team of licensed trades on staff, we take on everything from ground-up home builds and full remodels to specialty electrical, painting, and commercial work.

One company. Dirt to finishes. We handle the full build from start to finish with our own licensed electrician, licensed plumber, and finish crew. Every permit pulled, every inspection scheduled, no corners cut to save a few bucks.

Charlie's philosophy: "I like to build things and see satisfied customers." That's the whole business.

What We Build

One Company. Dirt to Finishes.

Remodels, painting, cabinetry, fencing, new builds, electrical, and more. Carried from start to finish by Cousin Charlie's Construction. Here's a few of the things we do.

Remodels & Additions

Kitchens, bathrooms, full home remodels, room additions, garage conversions. Cabinets, tile, plumbing, electrical, fixtures, finished clean.

Interior & Exterior Painting

Painting done the right way, inside and out. Proper prep, clean lines, even coats, and the right paint for the job. From a wall to a whole house.

Custom Cabinetry & Finish Work

Kitchen cabinets, vanities, built-ins, mantles, crown molding, trim. The finish details that turn a job from done to done right.

Fencing, Patios & Outdoor

Fences, slabs, driveways, walkways, patios, decks, sheds, and outdoor structures. Built and finished to hold up through Texas weather.

New Home Builds

Ground-up custom home builds. If you've got the plans and the property, we handle permitting, framing, and the build out from foundation to finishes.

Electrical Work

Licensed electrician on staff. Rewires, panel upgrades, lighting, kitchen and bath electrical, EV chargers. Permitted and inspected.
