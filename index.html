Who We Are

Family-Run. Fully Insured. Built on Doing It Right.

Cousin Charlie's Construction is a family-run construction company based in Friendswood, serving homeowners and businesses across Houston, Conroe, and Galveston. With 15 years of construction experience and a full team of licensed trades on staff, we take on everything from ground-up home builds and full remodels to specialty electrical, painting, and commercial work.

We do 90% of the work in-house with our own licensed electrician and licensed plumber on staff. The few specialty partners we bring in are licensed and insured. We pull every permit, get every inspection, and do not cut corners to save a few bucks.

Charlie's philosophy: "I like to build things and see satisfied customers." That's the whole business.

What We Build

From a Light Fixture to a Full Home Build.

Specialty trades on staff, full remodels in-house, and ground-up new builds when you've got the plans. Here's a few of the things we do.

Electrical Work

Licensed electrician on staff. Rewires, panel upgrades, lighting, kitchen and bath electrical, EV chargers. Permitted and inspected.

Painting & Finish Work

Interior and exterior. Prep done properly, clean lines, even coats, and the right paint for the job. From a wall to a whole house.

Plumbing

Licensed plumber on staff. Bath and kitchen reroutes, water lines, fixture installs, repairs. In-house, no waiting on outside trades.

Remodels & Additions

Kitchens, bathrooms, full home remodels, room additions, garage conversions. Cabinets, tile, plumbing, electrical, fixtures, finished clean.

Patios, Concrete & Outdoor

Slabs, driveways, walkways, patios, decks, sheds, and outdoor structures. Built and finished to hold up through Texas weather.

New Home Builds

Ground-up custom home builds. If you've got the plans and the property, we handle permitting, framing, and the build out from foundation to finish.
