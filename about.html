Our Story

Started With a Tool Belt. Grew Into a Company.

Charlie got his start working alongside family in an industrial setting, learning the trade the right way before ever swinging a hammer for himself. He started Cousin Charlie's Construction two years ago as a handyman business out of Friendswood, taking on the kind of small repair jobs most contractors won't touch.

Word got around. The jobs got bigger. Today the shop is a full-on construction company doing residential remodels, custom cabinets, patios, additions, and even commercial work. The crew has grown to three workers plus a few trusted specialty subs, all licensed and insured.

What hasn't changed: the philosophy. Show up when we said we'd show up. Pull every permit. Pass every inspection. Treat the client's home like our own.

"I like to build things and see satisfied customers. That's the whole reason I started this."

What Sets Us Apart

The Stuff Most Contractors Don't Bother With.

Anyone with a truck and a saw can call themselves a contractor in Texas. Here's what makes the difference.

OSHA 30 Certified

30 hours of formal construction safety training. Required for serious commercial work, voluntary for residential. We have it either way.

Permits & Inspections, Always

Every job, no matter the size. If it needs a permit, we pull it. If it needs an inspection, we schedule it. No shortcuts, no liability for you down the road.

Fully Insured

General liability and workers comp on every crew member. Your property is protected, our people are protected, you don't get a surprise lawsuit.

Licensed Electrician On Team

No outsourcing wiring jobs. We have a licensed electrician on the crew so kitchen, bath, and addition wiring is handled in-house.

Licensed Plumber On Team

Same story for plumbing. Bathroom remodels, kitchen reroutes, water lines. Done in-house by someone who's licensed to do it.

90% In-House Work

Most of the work happens with our own crew. The handful of specialty subs we bring in are licensed and insured. No subcontractor runaround.
