Our Story

Built on 15 Years. Backed by Licensed Trades.

Cousin Charlie's Construction was founded by Charlie, who brings 15 years of hands-on construction experience to every project. He got his start working alongside family in an industrial setting, learning the trade the right way before launching his own company. Today Cousin Charlie's Construction operates as a full-service construction company headquartered in Friendswood, with a team of licensed trades on staff and active projects across Houston, Conroe, Galveston, and the surrounding metro.

The company handles the full spectrum of construction work: ground-up new home builds, residential remodels and additions, specialty electrical and painting, custom cabinetry, fencing and outdoor builds, roofing, HVAC, and commercial tenant build-outs. A licensed electrician and licensed plumber on staff mean the major trades are run by our team, and every job is carried from start to finish under one company.

What sets us apart isn't size, it's how the work gets done. Every permit pulled. Every inspection scheduled and passed. OSHA 30 certified safety standards on every job site. The client's home treated like our own. That's the standard. We don't deviate from it.

"I like to build things and see satisfied customers. That's the whole reason I started this."

What Sets Us Apart

The Stuff Most Contractors Don't Bother With.

Anyone with a truck and a saw can call themselves a contractor in Texas. Here's what makes the difference.

OSHA 30 Certified

30 hours of formal construction safety training. Required for serious commercial work, voluntary for residential. We have it either way.

Permits & Inspections, Always

Every job, no matter the size. If it needs a permit, we pull it. If it needs an inspection, we schedule it. No shortcuts, no liability for you down the road.

Fully Insured

General liability and workers comp on every team member. Your property is protected, our people are protected, you don't get a surprise lawsuit.

Licensed Electrician On Team

A licensed electrician on staff means kitchen, bath, addition wiring, and full rewires are run by our team. No waiting on an outside electrician to get your job moving.

Licensed Plumber On Team

Same story for plumbing. Bathroom remodels, kitchen reroutes, water lines, fixture installs. Run by our team, licensed and ready to start when the job starts.

One Point of Contact

You hire us, we run the job. From the first estimate to the final walkthrough, you deal with Cousin Charlie's Construction. No middleman, no general contractor handoffs, no subcontractor runaround.
